- 3D scenes are code: `3d/scenes/<name>/build.py` on `3d/lib/ds3d.py`. Render with `3d/scripts/render.sh`.
- Never commit `brand.local/`, `audience.local/`, `.banned-terms.local`, or anything under `work/`.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Dreamstate brand only.
- Do not install, download, post, send, schedule or spend. Those need the owner's yes on that item.
- The Blender bridge is for Codex only, telemetry off. See `3d/mcp/README.md`.
