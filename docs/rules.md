# Rules

This repo holds design material. The rules for every seat, including what the Graphic Designer may connect and do, live in the gtm-team repo: [docs/rules.md](https://github.com/luisfigueroavale/dreamstate-gtm-team/blob/main/docs/rules.md). That file wins if anything here disagrees.

The parts that matter most for design:

- Nothing is posted, scheduled, sent or attached without the owner's yes on that exact item. A new attach can clear an earlier yes.
- No metric on an image without a source you can name.
- Portrait pixels only from photos the owner supplied. No generated face or body.
- Google Drive is read only.
- Local Blender runs on the owner's Mac, driven by scripts or by Codex through the Blender bridge, with telemetry off. No asset downloads or paid generation without the owner's yes.
- Credits are quoted before any paid step.
